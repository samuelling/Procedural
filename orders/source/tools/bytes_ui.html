Where the bytes go

Each rung is one page: a small unpacker, then everything else in one compressed stream. Every part is charged what it adds to that stream where it stands, so a part that repeats what came before it costs less. Inside a file, its share is divided among its definitions by how big each one is compressed on its own. The two smallest rungs are not compressed and are counted by hand, character by character. Counted by tools/bytes.mjs.
